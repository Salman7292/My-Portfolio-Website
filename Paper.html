Explainable Deep Learning Approach for ALL Diagnosis

This groundbreaking research introduces a novel diagnostic framework that synergizes the EfficientNet-B7 architecture with Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) methodologies to address challenges in performance, computational efficiency, and explainability for Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) diagnosis.

The proposed model achieves diagnostic accuracies exceeding 96% while reducing computational overhead by up to 40% compared to state-of-the-art architectures. Integrated XAI techniques (Grad-CAM, CAM, LIME, and IG) provide transparent insights into model decisions, enhancing trust among healthcare practitioners.

Research Questions Addressed

R1: Diagnostic Accuracy & Efficiency

How can the diagnostic accuracy of ALL prediction models be improved while maintaining computational efficiency for real-time clinical applications?

R2: XAI Integration Challenges

What are the key technical challenges in integrating XAI into ALL diagnosis, and how can these be overcome to enhance model explainability?

R3: Trust & Transparency

To what extent can XAI methodologies improve trust and transparency in DL-based diagnostic frameworks for medical practitioners and patients?

Future Research Directions

  • Exploring additional architectures (Randomized and Spiking Neural Networks)
  • Extending the framework to a broader spectrum of hematological diseases
  • Addressing scalability challenges using semi-supervised and unsupervised learning
  • Optimizing computational overhead introduced by XAI methods for real-time clinical use
  • Validating performance on other medical imaging domains beyond hematology
